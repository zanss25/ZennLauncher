# **Zenn Launcher**

#### Your Personal Unified Hub for Games and Tools.

Zenn Launcher is a lightweight, personalized launcher designed to bridge the gap between your game library and your essential modding/emulation tools. Tired of jumping between folders and different apps? Zenn brings everything into one sleek, customizable interface.

## Key Features

- **Unified Library:** Manage your Games and Tools (emulators, modding utilities) in separate, dedicated tabs.
- **Flexible Views:** Choose your style with Grid, Icons (WIP), or List view modes (WIP).
- **The "Relics" System (WIP):** Don't just delete games; immortalize them. Relics keep your stats, commentary, and history even after the game is uninstalled.
- **Advanced Personalization:**
  - **Profile System:** Multiple profiles with individual databases and password protection.
  - **Launcher Settings:** Custom startup behaviors, backdrop effects, and directory management.
  
- **Detailed Analytics:** Track play counts (WIP), "Last Launched" timestamps, and provide your own "User Commentary" for every entry.
- **Smart Management:** Effortlessly add, edit, or categorize your library with a robust Properties editor.

## Core Elements

#### Navigation Tabs

- **Games:** Centralized hub for managing your game library. Supports sorting by Alphabetical, Most Launched, and more.
- **Tools:** Dedicated space for non-game software like mod managers or emulators.
- **Categories (WIP):** Organized sections to split your library by genre or project.
- **Relics (WIP):** A digital trophy room for your gaming history.

#### User Settings

- **General:** Manage post-launch actions (e.g., auto-hide launcher) and Windows startup options.
- **Profile:** Backup your entire profile (data, stats, and settings) into a .zip file.
- **Appearance:** Customize the UI with backdrop effects and accent colors.
- **Folders:** Set default global paths for executables, covers, and icons to speed up adding new items.
- **Categories:** Customize categories for organizing your games and tools.
- **Advanced:** Debug and optimization options and other more technical settings.
